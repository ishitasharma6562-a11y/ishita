The Challenge

A brand famous for humour, flattened by its own digital experience.

Old Spice built its identity on bold, self-aware humour — but that personality rarely survives the jump from a 30-second ad to an everyday digital product. The gap between "iconic voice" and "generic interface" was the starting point for this exploration.

The Direction

Let the interface talk like the ads do.

The concept explores where personality can live in the product itself — microcopy, empty states, confirmation moments — rather than being confined to marketing. Small, well-placed moments of humour turn routine interactions into ones users actually enjoy.

Product UX doesn't have to be quiet to be usable.
