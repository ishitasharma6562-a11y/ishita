Sprint 01 PhysicsWallah — when a star teacher leaves EdTech · 2026 → 2031
Sector EdTech Focal actor PhysicsWallah Group sprint Ishita, Tanish, Thishya, Mahak, Yagna

A five-part sector sprint, 1.1 to 1.5 — from an ecosystem canvas of Indian test-prep EdTech, through the point where a star teacher leaving breaks the sale, to ten concept specifications for 2031.

Each card names the exact 2026 artifact it removes — material or digital — and the failure it belongs to: media break, redundant request, latency gate.

EdTech · Focal actor: PhysicsWallah · Workflow: JEE/NEET aspirant moving from free YouTube lectures to a paid batch · As of Oct 2026

4. Demand & Selection
USERS, FRICTIONS, SWITCHING COSTS
  • 22.8 lakh NEET + 16 lakh JEE Main applicants
  • Students use, parents pay
  • ~₹3,810 online vs ~₹32,000 offline per year
  • Cheap to switch in money, costly in time
  • Post-BYJU'S trust gap
  • Frictions: bandwidth, discipline, language
THE BOTTLENECK · BLOCK 1 × BLOCK 3

PW's AI tutor runs on minors' data, which the law is about to restrict

1 · Knowledge
AI Guru and personalised practice learn from how students use the app; ~80% of doubts already go to AI.
3 · Institutions
DPDP Rules require verifiable parental consent and bar behavioural monitoring of under-18s from ~May 2027. Most JEE/NEET aspirants are 16–17.
4 · Demand
AI cut doubt-solving cost to 5–7%, which keeps a batch near ₹4,000. Lose the data, lose the price.

Why it could break the ecosystem: if consent friction thins the data flywheel, PW loses its cost edge, the affordability promise breaks, and price-sensitive Tier 2/3 students fall back to free YouTube.

Watch: DigiLocker-based parental consent tokens, the educational-institution exemption, and whether PW reframes AI as a parent-visible service.

1.2Value Network Map

Where a teacher leaving breaks the sale.

The friction point
What fails
The student doesn't renew next year's batch and asks for a refund on this one.
What's missing
Loyalty to PW. The student trusted the teacher, not the brand. When the teacher left, that trust walked out too.
What it breaks
Fans follow the teacher to a rival · PW loses renewals and word of mouth · PW must pay more to keep star teachers · costs and prices go up.

In one line: PW owns the app, but the teacher owns the trust. When the teacher leaves, the students leave with them.

The artifact trail of the failure
1 · The contract binds lectures, not fans
The teacher's employment contract covers recorded lectures and a notice period. Nothing on paper covers the audience, which lives on the teacher's own channels.
2 · The YouTube channel carries the trust out
One "I'm leaving PW" video with a new-batch link and a coupon code moves the student's attention to the rival. The student's phone notification bell does the rest.
3 · PW's own artifacts go quiet
PW still holds the batch ID and the entitlement token, so access continues. But its "new faculty assigned" alert and renewal reminders get ignored.

1.3Actor Action Cards

Around the Stage 2 failure point: a star teacher leaves mid-year and students stop renewing · 3 human + 3 non-human actors

HUMAN
Student
PROBLEMATIZATION
I joined for my teacher, not for PW. If Sir leaves mid-year, my JEE/NEET preparation breaks and I may lose the year.
OPP GATE
Must stay in an active, paid batch taught by the same teacher who brought them in (teacher continuity).
ARTIFACTS
Material: Printed DPP sheets, notebooks, offline-centre ID card · Digital: Batch enrollment ID, watch history, doubt history, test scores
DISSIDENCE / FAILURE ACTION
Stops attending, skips renewal, raises a refund request and moves to the teacher's new platform.
HUMAN
Parent / Guardian
PROBLEMATIZATION
I paid for the teacher my child trusts. If that teacher is gone, PW has not kept its promise.
OPP GATE
Pays to renew only if the child still wants the batch and there is a clear refund path.
ARTIFACTS
Material: Printed fee receipt, batch brochure · Digital: UPI payment reference, invoice PDF, education-loan e-sign
DISSIDENCE / FAILURE ACTION
Refuses next year's fee, demands a refund, and complains online or on a consumer forum.
HUMAN
Star Teacher
PROBLEMATIZATION
Students come for me. PW earns the revenue but I bring the audience, so I deserve more pay and control.
OPP GATE
Contract terms (pay, creative control, notice period) must stay acceptable, or the teacher exits.
ARTIFACTS
Material: Signed employment contract, studio whiteboard · Digital: Recorded lecture files, PW faculty login, personal YouTube / Telegram channel
DISSIDENCE / FAILURE ACTION
Leaves mid-year, announces it on their own channel and takes the fan base to a rival.
NON-HUMAN
PW Batch & Enrollment System
PROBLEMATIZATION
Treats a batch as a product with a timetable. It can swap a teacher in one click but cannot transfer the students' trust.
OPP GATE
A valid paid entitlement plus an assigned faculty member for every scheduled class.
ARTIFACTS
Material: Offline-centre attendance register · Digital: Entitlement token, batch ID, class-schedule JSON, "teacher changed" push notification
DISSIDENCE / FAILURE ACTION
Silent faculty swap · timetable gaps and confused students · churn spike; renewal reminders are ignored.
NON-HUMAN
Payment & Refund Gateway
PROBLEMATIZATION
Only sees large transactions. A refund is a rule check, not a relationship.
OPP GATE
Verified UPI mandate / OTP for renewal; refunds only inside the policy window and conditions.
ARTIFACTS
Material: Printed invoice / fee receipt · Digital: Transaction ID, UPI autopay mandate, OTP log, refund ticket, chargeback record
DISSIDENCE / FAILURE ACTION
Autopay cancelled; refund denied · chargeback, consumer complaint and bad reviews.
NON-HUMAN
Teacher's YouTube Channel
PROBLEMATIZATION
The channel, not PW, holds the audience. The algorithm keeps pushing the teacher to subscribers wherever they teach.
OPP GATE
The teacher must own and control the channel and its subscriber base.
ARTIFACTS
Material: Student's smartphone · Digital: Subscriber list, notification alerts, "I'm leaving PW" video, new-batch link in the description
DISSIDENCE / FAILURE ACTION
One video redirects the fan base to the rival overnight, and PW's free funnel loses them. Blue tag = human actor · Violet tag = non-human (technical) actor

            Step 6 · Friction summary: what all five flows show
              1. Every · happens where paper meets PW's apps. PW is digital inside, but letters, notebooks, registers, receipts and signed forms still have to be typed in by someone.
              2. Every · asks for information PW (or a government app) already has. There's no single student profile that moves with them, so each step starts from zero.
              3. Most · are in the checking (Validation) stage. Starting is instant (a video, a click, a cancelled payment); waiting for a person to review is what slows things down and loses trust.
              4. Contextual Integrity: data shared inside PW mostly follows the rules. Problems appear when information leaves PW (the YouTube video, the rival sign-up) or when data about how under-18s study is used for more than it was collected for.
              5. Back to our Stage 2 problem: PW owns the access (logins, batches, payments), but the teacher owns the trust. Trust moves in hours (S1, S3, S5, $7); PW's own messages take days or weeks (S4, T4, $6).
              1.52031 Concept Specifications

              Ten innovation ideas, two per STEEP sector, each backtracked from a real 2026 artifact in our Stage 4 workflows.

              SOCIAL · IDEA 01

              Verified Teacher Handover Live

              2031 system role
              When a teacher leaves, the outgoing and incoming teacher run a live handover class for the batch inside the app. Students hear the reason first-hand and get a clear plan for the rest of the year.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              Rumour screenshots forwarded in Telegram / WhatsApp batch groups, and PW's late in-app notice (Workflow 1).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              A Hinglish live class in the familiar “bhaiya / didi” style, with both teachers on screen. Students ask questions in chat; the recording is pinned for anyone who missed it.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: PW app (both teachers) · enrolled batch students
              Subject · Info type: The batch · faculty-change reason, new timetable, transition plan
              Transmission principle: Shared only inside the enrolled batch; the leaving teacher agrees to what is said about their exit.
              SOCIAL · IDEA 02

              Family Decision Room

              2031 system role
              A shared space where the student and parent see progress together and decide on renewal as a family. It replaces one-way reminders with a joint decision.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              CRM renewal reminder that gets ignored; the parent decides without seeing the student's progress (Workflow 1).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              One shared family view on one phone. The parent gets a Hindi voice summary; the student adds a voice note on why they want to stay or switch. Renewal needs both taps.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: PW app · student + linked parent
              Subject · Info type: The student · attendance, test trend, teacher-continuity status (no private doubt chats)
              Transmission principle: Parent consent for minors under DPDP; the student chooses which progress items the parent sees.
              TECHNOLOGICAL · IDEA 03

              Portable Learning Passport

              2031 system role
              The student's verified learning record (scores, chapters done, doubt summary) lives in an APAAR-linked wallet and moves with them across batches and platforms. PW reads it with consent instead of asking again.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              Re-confirming batch and enrollment data PW already holds (Workflow 2).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              One “Share my progress” tap, protected by a PIN on the shared family phone. Shown as a simple chapter map, not a data dump.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: Student's learning wallet (DigiLocker / APAAR) · PW batch system or another platform
              Subject · Info type: The student · verified progress summary
              Transmission principle: Student-controlled, purpose-limited consent token that expires when the batch ends.
              TECHNOLOGICAL · IDEA 04

              Continuity AI Tutor

              2031 system role
              An AI tutor trained on the outgoing teacher's own lectures and notes covers the gap during a faculty swap. It keeps the same explanations and pace until the new teacher settles in.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              Class gap while the new teacher onboards, and a bare “new faculty assigned” push alert (Workflow 2).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              Same Hinglish style and worked examples, clearly labelled “AI, based on Sir's lectures”. Students ask doubts by voice.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: PW content system · AI tutor · student
              Subject · Info type: The teacher's lecture content · the student's doubts
              Transmission principle: Teacher's contract licenses lectures for AI use with credit and royalty; doubts are used only to answer them.
              ENVIRONMENTAL · IDEA 05

              Offline Classroom Box

              2031 system role
              A small device at each offline centre caches lectures, marks attendance by QR or student card, and syncs with PW when the internet returns. Paper registers and end-of-week retyping disappear.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              Paper attendance register retyped by hand: the manual media break (Workflow 3).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              Works with no internet. Students tap their ID card at the door like a metro gate; the centre manager sees a simple daily list in the local language.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: Centre box · PW central system
              Subject · Info type: Students at the centre · attendance time-stamps
              Transmission principle: Collected only for attendance and safety; synced encrypted; each parent sees only their own child's record.
              ENVIRONMENTAL · IDEA 06

              Voice & IVR Updates

              2031 system role
              Families without smartphones or with weak data get timetable and teacher-change updates as a voice call in their language. They press a key to confirm or ask for a callback.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              Printed timetable on the centre notice board that students miss (Workflow 3).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              Hindi, Bhojpuri or Marathi voice on any basic phone. Give a missed call to hear it again; no reading needed.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: PW notification service · parent's / student's phone number
              Subject · Info type: The batch · timetable and teacher change
              Transmission principle: Only to numbers registered at admission; opt out any time; no marketing in the same call.
              ECONOMIC · IDEA 07

              Teacher-Linked Fair Refund

              2031 system role
              Fees are paid month by month and tied to the promised teacher. If that teacher leaves, the unused amount is refunded automatically, with no ticket needed.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              Refund request ticket + · fee receipt screenshot + · manual review queue (Workflow 4).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              The parent gets a Hindi voice note: “Sir has left. ₹1,200 is coming back to your UPI today.” One tap: stay with the new teacher, or keep the refund.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: PW billing system · parent's UPI app
              Subject · Info type: The parent's fee · refund amount and reason
              Transmission principle: Fee terms the parent accepted at purchase; no new data or documents asked for.
              ECONOMIC · IDEA 08

              Trust Escrow

              2031 system role
              Part of the yearly fee sits in escrow and is released to PW month by month only while the promised teacher and timetable hold. Families feel safe paying upfront, and PW still gets steady income.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              One-shot annual UPI autopay mandate, and the fight to claw money back (Workflow 4).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              A simple “money safe” meter in the app shows how much is still protected, explained in Hindi like a familiar family savings kitty.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: Parent's bank · regulated escrow account · PW
              Subject · Info type: The parent's payment · release triggers (teacher continuity, classes held)
              Transmission principle: RBI-regulated escrow rules; release conditions written into the fee agreement.
              POLITICAL / REGULATORY · IDEA 09

              One-Tap Consent & Delete

              2031 system role
              Parents manage what PW can do with their child's data through a DPDP consent manager, with time-limited, purpose-tagged consent. Withdrawing consent or deleting data takes one tap, not emails and ID photos.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              ID photo sent again + · complaint email + · grievance-officer wait (Workflow 5).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              Plain-language consent cards with icons and a Hindi voice explainer. Shows “who used your child's data this month”.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: Parent's consent manager · PW data system
              Subject · Info type: The child · consent status and deletion request
              Transmission principle: DPDP Act consent and erasure rights, verified through the existing DigiLocker login (no new ID upload).
              POLITICAL / REGULATORY · IDEA 10

              Faculty Change Ledger

              2031 system role
              A regulator-visible ledger records every teacher promise, change and refund for each batch. Consumer authorities audit it automatically instead of waiting for complaints.
              2026 baseline friction resolved
              National Consumer Helpline complaint form, and “star teacher” ads that overpromise (Workflow 5).
              Cultural ergonomics & interaction model
              Before buying, parents see a public “batch promise card” (teacher, dates, refund rule). A complaint becomes one tap that points to the ledger entry.
              Contextual integrity audit
              Sender · Recipient: PW · regulator audit node (e.g. CCPA)
              Subject · Info type: Batches and faculty · change dates, refunds issued (no student personal data)
              Transmission principle: Statutory audit mandate; aggregated data only, no individual student records.
